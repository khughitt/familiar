Zero bytes means the process died between creating the temp and writing it -- a hook killed when its agent's turn is cancelled, which lock.js already documents as routine. Nothing ever sweeps them.

Fix direction: sweep `*.tmp.<pid>.*` in the state dir whose pid is dead (or older than a bound) from an existing housekeeping point such as pruneLedgers after a hook; harmless today but grows without bound.
